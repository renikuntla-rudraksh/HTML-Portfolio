THE BEST MOVIES ACCORDING TO ME

My top 3 movies of all time

1. Bahubali

An epic tale of a lost prince who discovers his royal legacy and fights to reclaim his kingdom.

2. Salaar

A violent action drama about friendship, power, and a man who rises to dominate a brutal crime empire.

3. Magadheera

A warrior’s love story that spans across two lifetimes, blending reincarnation and revenge.
